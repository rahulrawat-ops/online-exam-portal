Portal Guidelines & Capabilities

  1. Students: Log in with Roll Number to take scheduled examinations with a live timer and question palette.
  2. Administrators / Instructors: Log in with Admin ID to:
    • Create new questions (MCQs with 4 options, answer key, explanation).
    • Edit or update existing exam questions in real-time.
    • Delete outdated questions from the question bank.
    • Modify exam duration (timer limit) and paper title.
    • View recorded student test submissions and scores.
  3. Each question carries 1 Mark. Results are calculated instantly on submission.
  4. All custom questions and edits are automatically saved and immediately presented to students taking the exam.
